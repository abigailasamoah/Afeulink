The process

Simple. Fast.
Reliable.

01

Contact us for a quote

Tell us your destination, package weight and contents via WhatsApp or email. We respond within 2 hours with a full price including all fees.

02

Drop off or arrange collection

Bring your package to us in Temse, Belgium. Or we arrange a DHL pickup from your address — we'll let you know the collection fee upfront.

03

Pay and we ship

Pay by bank transfer, Wise or cash. We book through our DHL Express business account and send you the tracking number immediately.

04

Delivered in 2 days

Your package is delivered door-to-door by DHL Express. Track it in real time. We follow up to confirm safe delivery.
